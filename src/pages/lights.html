Every light, by its period.

Light lists sort by place, because a navigator knows roughly where they are. This list sorts by period, the number a stopwatch gives you first, so a light you have timed can be looked up by that number. Each strip shows the same twenty seconds, so lights in one group can be compared by eye: where their periods agree, the rhythm or the colour has to tell them apart.

Each real light has its own page, which reads its characteristic word by word and sets it beside the lights on the site it could be mistaken for. Standard marks link to the page that explains them. To read a light from your own timing instead, use the timer.

Strips are drawn with the site's default timings (see the notes), except where a page gives exact flash lengths. Quick and very quick lights are drawn at full speed here; the players on each page slow them where they would flash more than three times a second. Characteristics and their sources are on each light's page.
